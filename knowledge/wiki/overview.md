# Gap Inc. Company Overview

## About Gap Inc.

Gap Inc. (NYSE: GAP) is the largest specialty apparel company in the United States, founded in 1969 and headquartered in San Francisco. It operates a portfolio of four distinct brands — Old Navy, Gap, Banana Republic, and Athleta — serving a wide range of consumer segments from value shoppers to premium lifestyle buyers. Products are sold through approximately 3,569 store locations in roughly 40 countries (as of end of FY2024), franchise partnerships, and e-commerce channels. Online sales represent 38% of total net sales in FY2024.

## FY2024 Performance Summary

**Full-year net sales: $15.1 billion** (up 1% vs. FY2023; up ~2% excluding the impact of one fewer fiscal week due to the loss of the 53rd week). Comparable sales were positive in all four quarters for the first time in years. Key full-year metrics:

- **Gross margin: 41.3%** — expanded 250 basis points year-over-year, one of the highest levels in the past 20 years
- **Operating income: $1.1 billion** at a 7.4% operating margin, meaningfully higher than FY2023
- **Net income: $844 million** ($2.20 diluted EPS)
- **Free cash flow: $1.0 billion**; cash position ended at $2.6 billion (up 38% from prior year)
- **Market share gains in 8 consecutive quarters** through Q4 FY2024

## Strategic Themes Under CEO Richard Dickson

Richard Dickson joined as President and CEO in July 2023, arriving from Mattel where he oversaw the reinvigoration of Barbie, Hot Wheels, and Fisher-Price. His turnaround approach for Gap Inc. centers on four pillars: (1) maintaining financial and operational rigor, (2) reinvigorating the brands, (3) strengthening the operating platform, and (4) energizing company culture.

**Brand reinvigoration playbook** — Each brand operates against a structured playbook: building stronger brand identity and purpose, delivering trend-right product, amplifying through compelling storytelling and cultural marketing, and improving omnichannel experience. Dickson's Q4 FY2024 comment captures the tone: "Gap is back in the cultural conversation. This brand was built on strong product narratives with brilliant marketing, expressed through big ideas, and over the past year, each of these were reignited."

**Cost and margin discipline** — FY2024 operating expenses were down 2% year-over-year. Inventory management improved significantly, with a 15% YoY reduction in Q1 FY2024. Lower commodity costs drove 210 basis points of merchandise margin expansion for the full year.

**Media transformation** — In May 2024, Dickson announced Omnicom Media Group (OMG) as Gap Inc.'s new cross-brand media agency of record, replacing its prior setup. Gap also created a new Marketing Shared Services organization to centralize media intelligence and planning across all four brands. The stated goal: "working as one team and one company to deliver greater efficiencies and elevate each of our individual brands with culturally relevant narratives."

**Competitive context** — Analysts from Wells Fargo, Jefferies, and GlobalData tracked the turnaround throughout 2024. Wells Fargo's Ike Boruchow noted that Gap Inc. posted consecutive quarters of comp growth above 3% for the first time in seven years. Analyst Neil Saunders of GlobalData said of the Gap brand specifically: "Gap has broken the habit of being a company in permanent decline." Risks acknowledged heading into FY2025 include tariff exposure, supply chain costs, and continued execution uncertainty at Athleta and Banana Republic.
